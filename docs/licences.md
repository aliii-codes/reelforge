Stock voices offered by ReelForge are limited to ones with verified permissive sources:
`marius`, `javert` (CC0 voice donations) and `george`, `paul`, `michael` (VCTK, CC-BY-4.0 —
credit "VCTK corpus, University of Edinburgh"). Expresso-derived voices such as `cosette` are
**CC-BY-NC (non-commercial)** and are deliberately blocked.

## Attribution to include when publishing

When narration was produced with Pocket TTS, CC-BY-4.0 asks for attribution of the model.
Suggested line for the description: `Voice synthesized with Kyutai Pocket TTS (CC-BY-4.0).`
The generated `caption.txt` does not add it automatically — add it if you publish.

## Content generated by ReelForge

- Avatar ("Forge"), scene graphics and icons: original, drawn in code in `renderer/src/`.
- Background bed and whooshes: synthesized by `reelforge/rendering/sound.py` — no samples.
- Fonts: Lato (SIL OFL 1.1) from the system; DejaVu Sans fallback (free licence).
- Music files you supply with `--music` require `--music-licence "..."`; ReelForge records the
  text you provide but cannot verify it. Never assume a downloadable track is royalty-free.
